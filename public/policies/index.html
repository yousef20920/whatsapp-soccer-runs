The fine print

Prices, refunds & terms

NASORY Runs organizes pickup soccer games. Players pay online to save a spot in a game.

Prices

  • Each game shows its price per player before you pay.
  • The price covers your share of the field rental.
  • All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and include any tax charged on the field rental.

Refunds

  • You leave the game: you can leave up to 2 hours before kickoff and get your money back, minus a small cancellation fee (2.9% + 30¢ CAD).
  • Less than 2 hours before kickoff: no refund, since the field is already paid for.
  • The game is cancelled: everyone who paid gets a full refund, automatically.
  • The game fills up while you pay: you get a full refund right away.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows them in 5–10 business days.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Charges appear on your statement as NASORY RUNS.

Playing

  • Pickup soccer is a contact sport. You play at your own risk.
  • Follow the facility’s rules, and any waiver it asks players to accept.
  • Play fair and respect everyone on the field. Organizers can remove players who don’t.
