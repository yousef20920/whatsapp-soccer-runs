SORY Booker

Fills in Active Mississauga field bookings for you from SORY's Reserve links: every field, the date, times, attendees and event details. You still agree to the waivers, sign and pay yourself.

Install (one time, on a computer)

  1. Download SORY Booker
  2. Open the downloaded file to unzip it. You'll get a sory-booker folder.
  3. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the sory-booker folder.
  5. Sign in to Active Mississauga in the same browser.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Not on phones or Safari.

Booking a field

  1. In Discord, press Reserve on an /available result (organizers only).
  2. Click 🤖 Open the booking, filled in. Keep the tab open while SORY works; it takes about 20 seconds.
  3. When the green banner appears, tick the 2 waivers, sign, then Add to cart and pay.
  4. Back in Discord, run /confirm to post the game.

Updating (current version 1.0.3)

  1. Download the new zip above and unzip it over your old sory-booker folder.
  2. In chrome://extensions, press the reload ↻ button on SORY Booker.

1.0.3 tells you when it needs updating. 1.0.2 also answers the two "I acknowledge" questions, so you only tick the waivers, sign and pay. 1.0.1 fixed the number of attendees sometimes staying at 1.

SORY Booker only runs on Active Mississauga pages, only when you open a SORY link, and never sends your information anywhere.
